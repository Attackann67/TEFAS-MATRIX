# Yazisma Sablonlari

Kurallar: acilis nezaketi yok; rakamlar ORIJINAL para biriminde; kapanis kuyrugu yok; em-dash yok; "lazim" yok; ic Turkce metinde emir kipi yerine rica ("hazirlayabilir misin, lutfen"). Ust yonetim: -mistir/-maktadir, yuklem sonda, karar ust tarafta birakilir. Dis Ingilizce: kisa, kurumsal, SCF/ic mekanik ifsa edilmez.

## 1. Istirak sorgu maili (EN, resmi) - vadesiz sorgusu
Subject: {Entities} - {Month} cash position

Dear {name},

Please find attached the {Month} liquid asset positions of {entities}, based on group liquidity reporting.

As of {date}, {entity} held {ccy breakdown} in cash and demand accounts, corresponding to {x}% of its total liquid assets. {ikinci sirket cumlesi}

We kindly request the following:
1. The rationale for holding these balances in demand accounts rather than time deposits.
2. The portion of each balance required for operational purposes.
3. The intended treatment of the amounts exceeding operational needs{, including the availability of local time deposit placement - kisitli pb ise}.
4. Confirmation of whether the current position remains in line with the {date} figures.

Kind regards,
Arcelik Corporate Treasury - Cash Management

NOT: pool uyesi olmayan istirake "group cash pool" secenegi YAZILMAZ. Kapali/kisitli pb (EGP, MAD, RUB) icin "where transferability allows" yumusatmasi.

## 3. Teams hatirlatma (mail sonrasi)
Hi {name},
I shared the {date} positions of {entities} by e-mail with the files attached; summary below for quick reference:
{bullet blok}
Could you confirm whether there has been any significant change as of {new date}, and share your feedback on the points raised in the e-mail?
Thanks!

## 4. Mine Hanim ozet maili (TR, kurumsal)
Konu: Overdue Takibi - {kisi} Toplanti Ozeti ({kesit})

{Kisi} ile overdue konusunda bir toplanti gerceklestirdik. Ozeti asagida bilgilerinize sunariz.

**Beko Europe:** Beko Europe Management S.R.L. overdue bakiyesi {kesit} itibariyla {X} mn EUR olup {ay} ayinda {Y} mn EUR artis gostermistir (yeni overdue {a}, tahsilat {b}). {icerik}...

**Diger istirakler:** {tek paragraf, sirket basina bir cumle}

{Rutin/karar cumleleri, yuklem sonda}. Tarafimizda ise cash durumlarinin iletilmesini takiben guncel analiz calismasina baslanacaktir.

KURAL: resmi listedeki rakam ana rakamdir; turetilmis toplam "diger tuzel kisilikler dahil" etiketiyle ve ikinci planda.

## 5. Sartli onay cevabi (Hakan tipi "uygun mu" sorusuna)
Yaklasim ara cozum olarak kabul edilebilir + 3 sart kalibi:
1) mekanizma olculebilir olmali (aylik sonuc vs yeni borc; baseline mutabakat),
2) raporlama duzeltmesi (bloke nakit 10202/10205),
3) hizlandirici adimin takvimi (SBLC vb.).
Kapanis: "With these in place, no objection to proceeding."
